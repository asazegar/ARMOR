4. Research Pillars & Core Techniques

Pillar 1: Distributed Architecture

Distributed Telemetry Engine

Engineering a lightweight agent-based telemetry layer to automate monitoring of heterogeneous systems in real-time, eliminating single points of failure and central bandwidth bottlenecks.

→ Goal: Zero-latency full visibility across edge nodes
Pillar 2: Algorithmic Detection

Autonomous Threat Engine

Integrating Apache Flink Complex Event Processing and GraphSAGE GNNs to discover zero-day exploits, lateral movement, and privilege escalation patterns proactively without static rules.

→ Goal: Shift from reactive patching to proactive discovery
Pillar 3: Situational Awareness

Orchestration & Response

Combining real-time visualization with automated SOAR playbooks, allowing administrators to review threat briefings and execute rapid containment across the entire network instantly.

→ Goal: Minimize cognitive load & drastically reduce MTTR
